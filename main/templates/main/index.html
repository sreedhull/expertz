    Good to know

    Soundproofing vs acoustic treatment

    They sound alike but solve different problems. Most studios and theatres need both.

    Soundproofing

    Keeps sound out, or in.

    • Blocks noise travelling between rooms and from outside
    • Ideal for studios, theatres and private spaces
    • Uses isolation systems built into walls and ceilings

    Acoustic treatment

    Makes the room sound right.

    • Reduces echo and controls sound reflections
    • Improves clarity of speech and music
    • Uses acoustic panels on walls and ceilings

    Not sure what your space needs? Ask our team and we will tell you honestly.
